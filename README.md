### Install

1. Unzip the [latest release](https://github.com/Treys21/NightOwl/releases/latest).
2. In `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode** and click **Load unpacked**.
3. Pick the folder, click the owl, and enter your Sleeper username.

Everything runs in your browser. No account, no tracking. Something off? [Tell me](https://github.com/Treys21/NightOwl/issues/new/choose).
