**Live** · every play, scored for each league

**Lineups** · the best lineup before kickoff

**Bets** · lines and hit rates for your starters

**Draft** · your rankings inside the draft room

## Install

1. Download the zip from the [latest release](https://github.com/Treys21/NightOwl/releases/latest) and unzip it
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder
4. Click the owl icon and enter your Sleeper username

<br>

## Privacy

Runs entirely in your browser. No account, no tracking.
